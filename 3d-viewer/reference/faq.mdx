<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I lost the model on screen. How do I get it back?">
    Click the **Home** icon above the ViewCube to return to the default view, or press **F** to fit the whole model on screen. See [ViewCube and Home](/3d-viewer/navigation/viewcube-and-home).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Part of the model is missing. What happened?">
    A section plane or section box may be active, or some categories may be hidden. Turn off the section in [Section analysis](/3d-viewer/tools/section-analysis), and check that every eye icon is turned on in the [Model browser](/3d-viewer/model-data/model-browser).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My measurements show the wrong units.">
    Open the Measure tools, click **Measure Settings**, and change the **Unit type** and **Precision**. See [Measure settings](/3d-viewer/measure/measure-settings).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The viewer feels slow. What can I do?">
    Open **Settings** and turn on **Disable shadow effect** in the Configuration tab. See [Viewer settings](/3d-viewer/display/viewer-settings).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I see the data of a specific element?">
    Click the element in the model, then open **Properties** in the toolbar. See [Properties](/3d-viewer/model-data/properties).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I walk through the model?">
    Yes. Click **First Person** in the toolbar and use your keyboard to walk. See [First person navigation](/3d-viewer/navigation/first-person).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The view is tilted. How do I straighten it?">
    Click the **Home** icon above the ViewCube to reset the camera.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

Still need help? Contact the ASTRA IPT team through the **Support** tab.

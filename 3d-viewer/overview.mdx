The 3D Viewer lets you open the BIM models delivered for your project and explore them from any angle, without installing any software. You can move around the model, walk through it in first person, cut sections to see inside, take measurements, and review the data behind every element.

## Recommended learning path

If you are new to the 3D Viewer, follow these tutorials in order:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your first model">
    Learn where to find the 3D Viewer and how to open a model. See [Opening a model](/3d-viewer/get-started/opening-a-model).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Learn the interface">
    Get familiar with the toolbar and controls. See [Interface tour](/3d-viewer/get-started/interface-tour).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Practice navigation">
    Master orbit, pan, and zoom before moving on to the other tools. See [Orbit, pan, and zoom](/3d-viewer/navigation/orbit-pan-zoom).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Explore the tools">
    Try sectioning, measuring, and reviewing model data as you need them.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Keep the [keyboard shortcuts](/3d-viewer/reference/keyboard-shortcuts) page handy. Shortcuts make navigation much faster once you know the basics.
</Tip>

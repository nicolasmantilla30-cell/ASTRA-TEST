Edit a collectible when its information changes, for example when it moves to a new location or you add new files.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the collectible">
    On the Collectibles page, click the collectible you want to edit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Edit">
    At the top of the collectible, click **Edit**.

    <Frame>
      <img
        src="/images/collectibles/edit-button.png"
        alt="The Edit button at the top of an open collectible"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change the information">
    Update the fields you need in the **Description**, **Acquisition**, and **Media & Attachments** sections.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your changes">
    Save your changes to update the collectible.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  To change only the **Description / Notes**, you do not need to click **Edit**. Click the pencil icon next to **Description / Notes** in the **Collectible details** panel.
</Tip>

<Warning>
  Required fields marked with an asterisk (\*) cannot be left empty.
</Warning>

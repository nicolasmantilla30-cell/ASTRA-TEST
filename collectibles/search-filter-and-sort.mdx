When your project has many collectibles, use search, filters, and sort options to find the one you need. You can combine all three.

## Search

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click the search bar">
    On the Collectibles page, click the search bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type your search">
    Type a name, maker, asset ID, or type. The list updates to show the matching collectibles.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Filter

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the filters">
    On the Collectibles page, open the filters.

    <Frame caption="The filter options">
      <img
        src="/images/collectibles/filters.png"
        alt="The filter panel with Category, Status, Condition, Acquired from, and Acquired to fields"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your filters">
    Set any of the filters: **Category**, **Status**, **Condition**, **Acquired from**, and **Acquired to**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply the filters">
    Click **Apply** to show only the matching collectibles. To close without changes, click **Cancel**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Click **Reset filter** to clear every filter and see all your collectibles again.
</Tip>

## Sort

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the sort dropdown">
    On the Collectibles page, click the sort dropdown. The default option is **Last modified**.

    <Frame caption="The sort options">
      <img
        src="/images/collectibles/sort-options.png"
        alt="The sort dropdown open with its list of sort options"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a sort option">
    Click one of the options: **Last modified**, **Name (A–Z)**, **Artist**, **Brand**, **Designer (A–Z)**, **Category (A–Z)**, or **Acquisition date (newest)**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

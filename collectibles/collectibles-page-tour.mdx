The Collectibles page is where you see, find, and add every collectible in your project. This tour shows you what each part of the page does.

## Take the tour

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the total">
    At the top of the page, **All** shows the number of collectibles in the project.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Look at the cards">
    Each collectible appears as a card with its photo, name, artist, and category tag. Click a card to open the collectible.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the search bar">
    Use the search bar to find collectibles by name, maker, asset ID, or type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the filters and sort options">
    Use the filters to narrow the list, and the sort dropdown to change the order. The default order is **Last modified**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the view buttons">
    In the top right corner, use the view buttons to switch between map view, grid view, and list view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the Upload button">
    In the top right corner, click the green **Upload** button to add a new collectible.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Grid view is the default layout. Switch to list view when you want to compare many collectibles at once.
</Tip>

Export a collectible to PDF to get a one page summary you can print or send. The PDF includes the photo, name, status, category, quantity, and location of the collectible.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the collectible">
    On the Collectibles page, click the collectible you want to export.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Export PDF">
    At the top of the collectible, click **Export PDF**.

    <Frame>
      <img
        src="/images/collectibles/export-pdf-button.png"
        alt="The Export PDF button at the top of an open collectible"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the PDF">
    The PDF downloads to your computer. Open it to review the summary.

    <Frame caption="An exported collectible PDF">
      <img
        src="/images/collectibles/exported-pdf.png"
        alt="A one page PDF with the collectible photo, name, status, category, quantity, and location"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Check that the photo and details of the collectible are up to date before you export, since the PDF shows the current information.
</Tip>

Open a collectible to see its large image and all of its information in the **Collectible details** panel. From here you can also share, export, edit, or delete the collectible.

## Open a collectible

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click a collectible">
    On the Collectibles page, click the collectible you want to open.

    <Frame caption="An open collectible with the Collectible details panel on the right">
      <img
        src="/images/collectibles/collectible-details.png"
        alt="An open collectible showing the large image and the Collectible details panel"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check where you are">
    The path at the top shows the project, **Collectibles**, and the item name. Click the back arrow to return to the Collectibles page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Look at the image">
    Use the expand and zoom buttons to see the large image in more detail.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the details">
    The **Collectible details** panel on the right shows **Item Name / Title**, **Description / Notes**, **Artist**, **Brand**, **Designer**, **Internal Asset ID / Tag**, **Category**, **Quantity**, **Property / Location Reference**, **Status** (for example, On Display), and **Attachments**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download an attachment">
    In **Attachments**, click the download icon next to a file to download it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  To add or change the **Description / Notes**, click the pencil icon next to it.
</Tip>

## Show or hide the details panel

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Info">
    At the top of the collectible, click **Info**.

    <Frame>
      <img
        src="/images/collectibles/info-button.png"
        alt="The Info button at the top of an open collectible"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="See the larger image">
    The details panel closes and the image uses more of the screen. Click **Info** again to show the panel.

    <Frame caption="The collectible with the details panel closed">
      <img
        src="/images/collectibles/details-panel-closed.png"
        alt="An open collectible with the details panel hidden"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Buttons on a collectible

At the top of an open collectible you will find these buttons:

- **Share**: create a public link to the collectible
- **Export PDF**: download a one page PDF of the collectible
- **Info**: show or hide the details panel
- **Edit**: change the information of the collectible
- **Delete**: remove the collectible

title: "Delete a Collectible"
sidebarTitle: "Delete a collectible"
description: "Remove a collectible and all of its data from your project."
icon: "trash"
---

Delete a collectible when it no longer belongs in your project. To prevent mistakes, the platform asks you to confirm before deleting.

<Warning>
  Deleting a collectible cannot be undone. It removes all of the collectible data, including its information and attachments.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the collectible">
    On the Collectibles page, click the collectible you want to delete.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Delete">
    At the top of the collectible, click **Delete**. A confirmation window opens.

    <Frame>
      <img
        src="/images/collectibles/delete-button.png"
        alt="The Delete button at the top of an open collectible"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type DELETE">
    In the box, type **DELETE**.

    <Frame caption="The delete confirmation window">
      <img
        src="/images/collectibles/delete-confirmation.png"
        alt="The confirmation window with a box to type DELETE and the Delete button"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Delete** to remove the collectible.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Before you delete, use **Export PDF** or download the attachments if you want to keep a copy of the collectible.
</Tip>

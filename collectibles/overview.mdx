The Collectibles module in ASTRA IPT lets you keep a record of every collectible in your project. You can upload items with their photos and documents, find them quickly, review their details, and share or export them. This module walks you through each of these tasks.

## Open Collectibles

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the project menu">
    Open your project and go to the project menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Collectibles">
    In the project menu, click **Collectibles**. The Collectibles page opens and shows **All** followed by the number of collectibles in the project.

    <Frame caption="The Collectibles page with every collectible shown as a card">
      <img
        src="/images/collectibles/collectibles-page.png"
        alt="The Collectibles page showing collectible cards with photo, name, artist, and category tag"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What this module covers

- **Get Started**: open Collectibles and learn the layout of the page
- **Adding Collectibles**: upload a new collectible with its description, acquisition data, and files
- **Finding Collectibles**: search, filter, sort, and switch between grid, list, and map views
- **Managing Collectibles**: view details, edit, export to PDF, share, and delete collectibles
- **Reference**: answers to common questions

<Tip>
  New to the module? Start with the [Collectibles page tour](/collectibles/collectibles-page-tour) to learn where every control is.
</Tip>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I open Collectibles?">
    Open your project, go to the project menu, and click **Collectibles**. See [Collectibles overview](/collectibles/overview).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why can't I save a new collectible?">
    One or more required fields are empty. Fill in every field marked with an asterisk (\*). See [Upload a collectible](/collectibles/upload-a-collectible).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What file types can I upload?">
    You can upload JPEG, PNG, GLB, GLTF, OBJ, PDF, DOC, and DOCX files in **Media & Attachments**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I can't find a collectible. What can I do?">
    Search by name, maker, asset ID, or type, and click **Reset filter** to clear any active filters. See [Search, filter, and sort](/collectibles/search-filter-and-sort).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I see my collectibles on a map?">
    Click the map view button in the top right corner of the Collectibles page. See [Grid, list, and map views](/collectibles/grid-list-and-map-views).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do people need an account to see a shared collectible?">
    No. Anyone with the public link can view the collectible without signing in. Click **Unpublish** to stop sharing. See [Share a collectible](/collectibles/share-a-collectible).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I recover a deleted collectible?">
    No. Deleting a collectible cannot be undone and removes all of its data. See [Delete a collectible](/collectibles/delete-a-collectible).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

Still need help? Contact the ASTRA IPT team through the **Support** tab.
